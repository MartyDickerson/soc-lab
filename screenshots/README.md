## Screenshot Tips

- **Blur sensitive data** — passwords, tokens, IPs (use your image editor)
- **Use full screen** — captures more context
- **Use dark mode** where available — looks better in documentation
- **Add annotations** — circle or arrow to highlight key areas

---

## Tools for Screenshots

- **Windows:** `Win + Shift + S` for snipping tool
- **Ubuntu:** `gnome-screenshot` or `scrot`
- **Annotation:** Greenshot, ShareX (Windows) or Flameshot (Linux)
